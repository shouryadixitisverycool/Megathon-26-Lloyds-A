## 1. Classifier Calibration at Scale — what is implemented, and what should change?

### Takeaway

**Overall: Partial.** Validation-fitted binary Platt/isotonic calibration and held-out before/after diagnostics are implemented, but the paper's recommended Beta/Venn–Abers alternatives and broader benchmark are not. **Important identity correction: this paper benchmarks binary classification, not multiclass calibration**; four independent product-adoption outcomes are not a mutually exclusive multiclass task. — [Primary abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.19944); [local paper, lines 7–29, 1711–1788](../../research-papers/classifier-calibration-at-scale.md); [training, lines 139–246](../../src/greenpath/models/propensity/training.py).

### Cited Findings

- **Identity:** Valery Manokhin and Daniel Grønhaug, *Classifier Calibration at Scale: An Empirical Study of Model-Agnostic Post-Hoc Methods*, arXiv:2601.19944v1, submitted 19 January 2026. Original abstract and HTML methods were fetched; the entire 2,157-line local transcription was read. Author code/artifacts are linked from the original paper. — [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.19944); [original full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.19944v1); [author release](https://github.com/valeman/classifier_calibration/tree/release-v1.0); [local transcription](../../research-papers/classifier-calibration-at-scale.md).
- **Actual method:** empirical comparison of 21 classifiers on 30 binary TabArena-v0.1 tasks, one randomized stratified five-fold outer CV, separate calibration samples drawn from training folds, and untouched test folds. Candidates: isotonic regression, Platt scaling, three-parameter Beta calibration (`abm`), Venn–Abers, and Pearsonify. Brier/log-loss are proper scoring rules; ECE/ECI and Spiegelhalter Z are additional diagnostics described in the paper. Section 5.2.5 specifically foregrounds Brier, log-loss, ECI, ROC-AUC, accuracy, CPU time and peak RAM; its ECE/ECI binning uses monotonic equal-mass sweep, not GreenPath's equal-width ECE. — [Methods §4.5 and §5.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.19944v1#S5.SS2); [local lines 819–935, 1739–1788](../../research-papers/classifier-calibration-at-scale.md).
- **Result to integrate, not promise:** Beta and Venn–Abers are sensible starting points, not universal winners. Platt/isotonic may worsen proper scoring for strong tabular predictors. The experiment changes both training-set size and the calibrator in some comparisons; the paper explicitly acknowledges this attribution confound and lacks significance tests establishing a universal best method. It omits distribution shift from its i.i.d. study. — [Conclusion](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.19944v1#S3); [local lines 279–322, 652–653, 1849–1860](../../research-papers/classifier-calibration-at-scale.md).
- **Implemented:** `Calibrator._fit_method` uses monotonic `IsotonicRegression` or near-unregularized logistic regression on `logit(raw_probability)`. `Calibrator.fit` calculates two-fold OOF Brier for both when each class has at least two examples, but automatic isotonic selection is allowed only when each class has at least 50 examples; otherwise Platt is forced. Ties prefer Platt. It refits on all supplied validation scores, clips to `[0.001, 0.999]`, creates fitted-data-sensitive versions, and derives reliability-bin gaps from OOF predictions when possible. This is real sigmoid/isotonic calibration, not temperature/vector/Dirichlet scaling and not a Venn guarantee. — [calibration.py, lines 25–125](../../src/greenpath/models/propensity/calibration.py).
- **Implemented with protocol caveat:** `train_product` fits learners on training customers, tunes logistic C/selects deployment using validation raw AUC/Brier, calibrates each candidate using validation, and chooses maximum-F1 thresholds on **in-sample calibrated validation predictions**. The same remaining validation cohort is reused for learner tuning, calibrator fitting/selection and threshold choice; this is not the paper's clean separately held-out calibration protocol. Test customers are excluded, and `confidence_release` is reserved before this work. — [training.py, lines 169–246](../../src/greenpath/models/propensity/training.py); [run.py, lines 208–245](../../src/greenpath/pipelines/run.py).
- **Implemented evaluation:** `model_metrics` reports calibrated Brier/ECE, discrimination/ranking/threshold metrics and uncalibrated Brier/ECE for logistic and boosted candidates; `stage_evaluate` writes before/after curves, subgroup gaps, missingness, generator-shift and temporal diagnostics. `metrics.summary` does not include log-loss, Spiegelhalter Z or ECI; calibration error uses ten equal-width bins, unlike the paper's MSM bins. The base learner is the same for before/after GreenPath comparisons, so those deltas isolate the post-processing more cleanly than comparisons involving retraining on smaller samples. — [ml.py, lines 82–92, 198–281](../../src/greenpath/evaluation/ml.py); [metrics.py, lines 211–257, 295–319](../../src/greenpath/evaluation/metrics.py).
- **Saved evidence, not a new training run:** all four selected models are LOGISTIC with Platt calibration. Validation positive counts are only EV 16, Green Home 17, Solar 17, Paperless 10, so **all four use the small-sample Platt fallback**, even though Solar's stored isotonic OOF Brier is lower (0.04307 vs Platt 0.05037). Below are selected-model held-out values extracted from the saved JSON; the direction of change matters more than rounded model-card prose. — [ml_evaluation.json, `products.*.counts/calibration/test.LOGISTIC`](../../reports/ml_evaluation.json); [selection rule, lines 90–108](../../src/greenpath/models/propensity/calibration.py).

- **Test evidence (inspected, not executed):** `test_calibrator_uses_only_validation_rows`, `test_calibrator_method_choice_and_local_error`, `test_selection_and_threshold_never_touch_test_rows`, `test_calibrated_probabilities_valid_and_monotone`, and `test_calibration_identity_includes_candidate_and_fitted_data` cover split isolation, fallback, output behavior and versions. They do not establish comparative Beta/VA improvement, independent calibration-set separation, or statistical calibration in deployment. — [test_propensity.py, lines 222–315](../../tests/ml/test_propensity.py).
- **Not implemented:** Beta, Venn–Abers and Pearsonify are absent from the calibrator's method dispatch; there is no no-calibration/identity option in automatic choice. **Not applicable to current product formulation:** multiclass-specific methods, explicitly a future extension of the paper rather than its benchmark. **Not necessary for a submission integration:** replicating all 21 learners/30 TabArena tasks. — [calibration.py, lines 35–49, 90–108](../../src/greenpath/models/propensity/calibration.py); [paper's future work, lines 1945–1959](../../research-papers/classifier-calibration-at-scale.md).
- **Guarantee warning:** the local paper's claim that VA bounds are guaranteed to contain an individual true probability is too strong to carry into product copy. The foundational Vovk/Petej/Fedorova abstract explicitly says merging imprecise outputs into one probability **loses the theoretical perfect-calibration property**. A merged VA score must not automatically become the product's validated correctness confidence. — [local lines 906–926](../../research-papers/classifier-calibration-at-scale.md); [foundational primary abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.00213).

4. **Acceptance:** test mutation of test/release labels cannot alter fitted calibrators/thresholds; verify finite bounded deterministic outputs, serialization, identity preservation, sparse/single-class failure policy, and monotonicity for methods advertised as monotone. Passing means an auditable comparison and safe non-degrading fallback policy—not a forced gain or confidence release. — Existing regression locations: [test_propensity.py](../../tests/ml/test_propensity.py), [test_selection.py](../../tests/trust/test_selection.py); methodological basis: [paper §5.2–5.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.19944v1#S5.SS2).

A table showing mixed outcomes is more credible than “calibration improves accuracy.” **Estimated effort:** small (about half–one engineering day) for paired log-loss/identity reporting; medium (roughly two–four days plus authorized regeneration/review) for Beta/VA candidates and clean split/cross-fit protocol. ## 2. Explainable AI for Trees — local SHAP is present; global understanding is missing

### Takeaway

**Overall: Partial.** Exact native Tree SHAP is implemented for a selected XGBoost model, and independent linear SHAP for logistic deployment, with raw-margin additivity checks. But the saved run selected logistic for every core product; there are no implemented global SHAP summaries, dependence/interaction analyses, explanation embeddings or SHAP-loss monitoring. — [auditor](../../src/greenpath/models/propensity/shap_audit.py); [saved ML JSON](../../reports/ml_evaluation.json); [original contributions](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.04610).

### Cited Findings

- **Identity:** Scott M. Lundberg, Gabriel Erion, Hugh Chen, Alex DeGrave, Jordan M. Prutkin, Bala Nair, Ronit Katz, Jonathan Himmelfarb, Nisha Bansal and Su-In Lee; *Explainable AI for Trees: From Local Explanations to Global Understanding*, arXiv:1905.04610v1, 11 May 2019. Original abstract/HTML methods were fetched and the entire 2,905-line local transcription read. Cite this exact preprint/title rather than silently substituting a later retitled publication. — [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.04610); [original full text](https://arxiv.org/html/1905.04610v1); [author study code](https://github.com/suinleelab/treeexplainer-study); [local paper](../../research-papers/explainable-ai-for-trees-global-understanding.md).
- **Method:** Tree SHAP computes exact Shapley feature attributions for a specified tree-traversal expectation game in polynomial time, worst-case `O(T L D²)`, with local accuracy `base + Σφ = model output`, consistency and missingness. “Exact” refers to that game, not proof of causal effects or knowledge of the true joint feature distribution. SHAP interaction matrices separate diagonal main effects from symmetric off-diagonal pair interactions; their row sums recover feature SHAP, and the matrix plus base recovers output. Global tools aggregate local explanations into mean absolute importance, beeswarms, feature-dependence plots, interaction summaries, explanation-space clustering/PCA and loss-attribution monitoring. — [Methods 9–10](https://arxiv.org/html/1905.04610v1#S10); [Methods 13–18](https://arxiv.org/html/1905.04610v1#S13); [local lines 930–1148, 1573–1857](../../research-papers/explainable-ai-for-trees-global-understanding.md).
- **Implemented exact tree-specific method, not just conceptual alignment:** `audit_shap` calls `booster.predict(DMatrix(x), pred_contribs=True, approx_contribs=False)` and separately requests `output_margin=True`. The chosen game is tree-path-dependent using fitted training cover; no external `shap.TreeExplainer` package is needed because XGBoost supplies native SHAP. Its API documents contribution sums equal raw untransformed margin and already supports `pred_interactions=True`. — [shap_audit.py, lines 35–48](../../src/greenpath/models/propensity/shap_audit.py); [official XGBoost 3.1 API](https://xgboost.readthedocs.io/en/release_3.1.0/python/python_api.html#xgboost.Booster.predict); [pinned XGBoost 3.1.1](../../pyproject.toml).
- **Implemented complementary method:** logistic deployment uses `(transformed_x - transformed_training_mean) * fitted_coefficients`, including missing indicators; baseline is intercept plus background mean dot coefficients. This is independent/interventional **linear SHAP on transformed feature space**, not the tree algorithm, not attribution through raw-feature imputation dependencies. Background statistics are fit on training rows and stored in the bundle. — [shap_audit.py, lines 19–34](../../src/greenpath/models/propensity/shap_audit.py); [training.py, lines 68–69, 223–224](../../src/greenpath/models/propensity/training.py).
- **Correct output-space boundary:** both branches enforce raw log-odds additivity (`atol=2e-5`) and serialize `output_space="raw_uncalibrated_log_odds"`, `explains_calibrated_probability=false`, baseline, attributions and residuals. The paper itself warns that nonlinear transformations cannot be handled by simply adjusting original Shapley values; in GreenPath the sigmoid/isotonic calibrator and probability clipping are not explained by these raw-margin values. Do not apply sigmoid independently to each SHAP value or call them percentage-point contributions. — [auditor, lines 46–57](../../src/greenpath/models/propensity/shap_audit.py); [paper Methods 10.2](https://arxiv.org/html/1905.04610v1#S10.SS2); [calibrator](../../src/greenpath/models/propensity/calibration.py).
- **Serving/evidence:** `/customers/{customer_id}/shap/{product}` returns a trained local audit when possible, otherwise explicit `NOT_AVAILABLE`, not reason codes renamed SHAP. Evaluation writes only `rows.head(100)` per product. The four saved `reports/shap/*.json` each contain 100 rows, all `exact_linear_interventional`, maximum stored residual below `1.8e-15`; this is persisted logistic audit evidence, **not four deployed tree-model demonstrations**. — [api.py, lines 375–391](../../src/greenpath/extensions/api.py); [ml.py, lines 215–220](../../src/greenpath/evaluation/ml.py); [EV audit](../../reports/shap/ev_finance.json), [Home audit](../../reports/shap/green_home.json), [Solar audit](../../reports/shap/solar_finance.json), [Paperless audit](../../reports/shap/paperless_banking.json).
- **Do not conflate customer reasons with SHAP:** `logistic_contributions` is uncentered coefficient-times-transformed-value for reason-code ordering, even when a tree is selected; it drops missing-indicator terms. It is not the complete deployed-model SHAP auditor, and synthetic heuristic/rule explanations are not SHAP. — [training.py, lines 249–260](../../src/greenpath/models/propensity/training.py); [provider.py](../../src/greenpath/models/propensity/provider.py); [API unavailable wording](../../src/greenpath/extensions/api.py).
- **Tests (inspected, not executed):** `test_deployed_shap_additivity_background_and_persistence` parametrizes both logistic and boosted branches, including an all-missing input, verifies JSON equality and raw-margin additivity/background; the logistic case checks missing-indicator names. Integration test `test_shap_and_portfolio_filters_are_exposed` checks endpoint units and residual. These establish intended branch coverage, not brute-force Shapley equivalence or global-analysis validation. — [test_propensity.py, lines 289–306](../../tests/ml/test_propensity.py); [test_traceability_lifecycle.py, lines 175–180](../../tests/integration/test_traceability_lifecycle.py).
- **Not implemented:** native interaction requests, SHAP-based mean-absolute summaries/beeswarms/dependence, supervised explanation-space clustering/PCA, or per-feature model-loss attribution. `feature_drift` computes missingness/median/KS of input distributions; that is **conceptually related monitoring, not the paper's supervised SHAP-loss monitoring**. Existing portfolio segments/aggregates and subgroup score metrics are not global SHAP. — [complete auditor](../../src/greenpath/models/propensity/shap_audit.py); [evaluation SHAP export, lines 215–220 and `feature_drift`, lines 121–141](../../src/greenpath/evaluation/ml.py); [paper §2.7](https://arxiv.org/html/1905.04610v1#S2.SS7).
