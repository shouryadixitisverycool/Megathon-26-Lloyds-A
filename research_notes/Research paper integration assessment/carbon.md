#### Actual method, results and limitations

- The study uses **2018 transactions from 101,744 adult Lloyds Banking Group customers**, initially about **62 million transactions**, compared with **5,473 LCFS households**. Its primary-account inclusion criteria include at least 12 customer-initiated transactions **in every month**, at least one energy payment, and restrictions on transfers/payments to other providers. These are study sample-completeness controls, not credit-eligibility rules and not a universal prescription for every banking application. [Local §2, §2.1–2.2.2, lines 209–310](../../research-papers/carbon-footprints-from-financial-transactions.md#L209).
- The emission factors are **consumption-based UKMRIO GHG multipliers**, including international/domestic supply chains and relevant direct fuel-burning emissions. For COICOP category `k`, `m_k = category kg CO2e / category household spend in GBP`; the study derives multipliers for **307 COICOP categories**, rolled into 12 broad consumption domains. These are **kg CO2e/GBP**, not kg CO2e/kWh or kg CO2e/litre. [Local §2.3, lines 316–370](../../research-papers/carbon-footprints-from-financial-transactions.md#L316).
- The bank's **252 TCS categories** are mapped to COICOP. A one-to-one match inherits its multiplier; one-to-many matches use LCFS household-spend weights: `m_TCS = sum_k(w_k * m_k)`, `sum(w_k)=1`. For bundled Gas & Electricity the paper describes a spend-weighted multiplier rather than claiming knowledge of the customer's actual energy split. It excludes **55 out-of-scope non-consumption categories** (26% of sample spending) and models domestic ATM cash spending using top cash categories and population cash-spend proportions. [Local §2.4, lines 374–414](../../research-papers/carbon-footprints-from-financial-transactions.md#L374).
- For each individual, `E_category = spend_category * multiplier_category`; category emissions are summed into broad COICOP domains and then annual footprint totals. This is an **estimated consumption footprint level**, not an intervention's avoided-emissions difference. [Local §2.5, lines 418–436](../../research-papers/carbon-footprints-from-financial-transactions.md#L418).
- Validation compares transaction and survey **distributions**, including housing, transport, food and age/income/region breakdowns. Joint-account holders are compared with household profiles; sole-account holders with equivalized individual survey profiles. The paper finds broad correspondence, not direct measurement of each customer's true emissions or proof that the same method transfers unchanged to another country/year. [Local §3, lines 440–667](../../research-papers/carbon-footprints-from-financial-transactions.md#L440); [verified abstract](https://wrap.warwick.ac.uk/id/eprint/169598/).
- The paper explicitly flags incomplete multi-bank spending, missing cash/basket detail, household-versus-individual allocation and **price-versus-volume proportionality uncertainty**. Supermarket payments include non-food purchases; spending more can reflect price rather than more physical consumption. Hybrid methods and missing-data inference are proposed as future improvements, not already experimentally resolved by this paper. [Local §3.1.4](../../research-papers/carbon-footprints-from-financial-transactions.md#L553); [§4.2–4.4, lines 751–843](../../research-papers/carbon-footprints-from-financial-transactions.md#L751).
- A source caution: the local text's explanation of KS endpoints at lines 448–454 conflicts with its subsequent correct interpretation that **low** KS statistics indicate close distributions at lines 468–469. A future implementation must use the conventional two-sample maximum-CDF-distance definition (zero for identical distributions), not copy the reversed explanatory sentence. This is a flagged source inconsistency, not a newly computed paper statistic. [Local §3](../../research-papers/carbon-footprints-from-financial-transactions.md#L448).

### Inferences: concrete staged implementation plan

The following are **recommendations**, not observed implementation or promised accuracy. They adapt the supplied paper's spend/mapping/coverage method to the existing classifier, schema and physical-engine boundaries. [Paper method](../../research-papers/carbon-footprints-from-financial-transactions.md#L316); [current schema](../../src/greenpath/pipelines/schemas.py#L72); [current impact engine](../../src/greenpath/extensions/uk_impact.py#L76).

1. **Stage T0 — preserve the method distinction and scope (small, ~0.5–1 developer day).** Add a documented contract proposal, e.g. `contracts/footprint.py`, defining a `ConsumptionFootprintResult` separate from intervention `ImpactResult`. Fields should include observed period, coverage status, consumption domains, method/factor/mapping versions, currency and price year, kg CO2e, counted/excluded/unmapped spend, household/account scope, evidence lineage and explicit limitations. Do not reuse current baseline-minus-scenario validators for a footprint level. Keep UK and India method releases separate. Acceptance: a consumption-footprint result cannot be passed off as an EV/Solar savings result or silently summed into recommendation impact. [Existing impact invariant, impact_detail.py:145–200](../../src/greenpath/contracts/impact_detail.py#L145).
2. **Stage T1 — reviewed monetary catalogue and mapping (medium, ~2–4 days engineering plus external data review).** Add proposed `impact/factors/consumption_multipliers.<edition>.json` and `impact/consumption_mapping.py`. Obtain a licensed/public UKMRIO-compatible consumption dataset and survey expenditure weights, pin source bytes/row locators/checksums, price year, purchaser-price/tax conventions, direct/indirect boundary and original currency. Build `m_k=E_k/S_k`; reject absent/nonpositive denominators. Map each bank category to fine COICOP codes with documented nonnegative weights summing to one. A frozen broad weighted category may be acceptable for a narrow prototype; OTHER must remain unmapped rather than get an invented universal factor. Version the classifier/contract if finer categories are added; changing the frozen enum without coordinating its consumers is not a safe shortcut. Acceptance: exact one-to-one and one-to-many fixture results, weight normalization, currency/year incompatibility failure, correct kg CO2e/GBP dimensions and no physical-factor substitution. [Paper §2.3–2.4](../../research-papers/carbon-footprints-from-financial-transactions.md#L326); [enum version policy](../../src/greenpath/contracts/enums.py#L1).
3. **Stage T2 — consumption eligibility and coverage pipeline (medium, ~2–3 days).** Add proposed `pipelines/consumption.py` operating on original transactions rather than averages in Customer360. Normalize spend into catalogue currency/price year only with explicit documented FX/deflator data; apply event and ingestion cutoffs; distinguish purchase, refund, transfer, repayment, fees and cash. Extend source schemas for linked refunds/transfers, account coverage and sole/joint/household scope where available. Keep unknown classifications/refunds in review instead of asserting credits are negative consumption. Return categorized observed subtotal and unmapped-spend share; do not label a partial-year/account view a complete annual household footprint. Cash may use a disclosed population-weighted estimate only with reviewed weights and a separate estimation flag. Acceptance: no transfer/card-repayment double count; refunds do not subtract unrelated salary credits; missing account/month/cash coverage is not zero; future/late data is excluded. [Paper sample/exclusions](../../research-papers/carbon-footprints-from-financial-transactions.md#L250); [current debit-only treatment](../../src/greenpath/pipelines/customer360.py#L107).
4. **Stage T3 — accounting, uncertainty and safe hybridization (medium, ~2–4 days after factors).** Implement proposed `impact/consumption.py`: `E_t = A_t * m(category_t)`, grouped by customer/COICOP domain/period/method. Emit an observed-spend estimate, not a causal or verified footprint. For a hybrid physical refinement, replace the corresponding operational component of a spend estimate only if its boundary can be decomposed; retain relevant upstream/product components, and never add full physical energy emissions on top of a multiplier that already includes them. Keep category-assignment, basket-allocation, missing-account, price-proxy and factor uncertainty separate. Use sourced factor ranges and calibration-supported class probabilities where available; shared factors must be drawn once per Monte Carlo replicate for all affected transactions so aggregate systematic uncertainty is not falsely diversified away. If no defensible distribution exists, publish a sensitivity range or `uncertainty_not_quantified`, not a fabricated 95% CI. Acceptance: hand-computed domain totals reconcile with customer subtotal; no overlap/double-counting; widening supported factor uncertainty does not narrow intervals; missing probabilities/ranges are disclosed. [Paper uncertainties/hybrid proposal](../../research-papers/carbon-footprints-from-financial-transactions.md#L797); [current scalar factors](../../src/greenpath/contracts/impact_detail.py#L61).
5. **Stage T4 — independent validation and optional surface (large/dependency-bound, ~3–5 days engineering; real data access longer).** Add proposed `evaluation/footprint.py`, `tests/impact/test_consumption.py` and an opt-in API/customer view showing domain estimates **next to**, not as, product potential reductions. Validate subtotal/mapping arithmetic on independently annotated transactions; preserve merchant-group and temporal holdouts; evaluate spend/emissions-weighted classification errors, category coverage, household/account completeness and distribution agreement with a compatible survey. With access to paired physical/receipt data, assess per-domain bias/MAE and breakdown by household/sole account, energy payment type, observed-month and missingness groups. Use conventional KS distance and bootstrap customer/household confidence bounds; survey equivalization only with explicit household composition. Acceptance: every displayed number traces to transaction, mapping and factor; all excluded/unmapped spend is reconciled; benchmark datasets use matched year, currency, population and boundary; metrics are reported with sample support and uncertainty. Set real-world error thresholds only after a baseline/pilot, not by copying paper accuracy. [Paper validation distinctions](../../research-papers/carbon-footprints-from-financial-transactions.md#L440); [existing merchant-group evaluation](../../src/greenpath/evaluation/transaction.py#L83).

**Submission payoff:** This would turn an opportunity-recommendation demo into a defensible consumption-domain profiling prototype, identify actual emissions hotspots rather than spending propensity alone, and show clear integration of Trendl's accounting method. A smaller credible submission can instead explicitly label T1–T4 deferred and demonstrate categorisation/coverage artifacts plus the physical-method distinction. The largest dependencies are reviewed monetary factors/mapping weights and representative consented banking/household data; synthetic labels alone cannot prove real footprint accuracy. [Paper data restrictions](../../research-papers/carbon-footprints-from-financial-transactions.md#L931); [current synthetic report limitation](../../reports/TRANSACTION_CATEGORISATION.md#L69).

## 2. Does GreenPath implement González-González et al.'s explainable automatic transaction-carbon method?

### Takeaway

Grounded recommendation prose and propensity SHAP are adjacent capabilities, not evidence that this transaction-XAI method has been implemented. ### Cited Findings

#### Identity and independently verified original methods

- **Jaime González-González, Silvia García-Méndez, Francisco de Arriba-Pérez, Francisco J. González-Castaño and Óscar Barba-Seara, “Explainable Automatic Industrial Carbon Footprint Estimation From Bank Transaction Classification Using Natural Language Processing,” IEEE Access 10, 126326–126338 (2022), DOI 10.1109/ACCESS.2022.3226324.** Local header gives publication 1 December 2022. The arXiv author-deposited version was submitted **23 May 2024**, and its record explicitly links the IEEE DOI; 2024 is the deposit date, not the original journal publication year. [Local header](../../research-papers/explainable-carbon-footprint-estimation.md#L1); [arXiv metadata and related DOI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.14505).
- **Verification:** retrieved the author-deposited full HTML and checked §§III–IV methods, equations and Tables IV–VI against the supplied extraction. IEEE direct page requests did not return usable article content in this session. The valid IEEE record found by title is document **9968254**; an initial speculative document request **9964266** yielded no useful evidence and is not cited as this paper. The local file is a full article extraction with collapsed words/interleaved columns and absent table bodies; clean author HTML provides the readable original methods and tables. [Author full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.14505v1); [IEEE record](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9968254); [local full extraction](../../research-papers/explainable-carbon-footprint-estimation.md#L138).
- **Data and classifier:** 25,853 Spanish bank transactions from CoinScrap, deduplicated/downsampled to 2,619 archetypes by discarding descriptions with similarity greater than 90%. Eight combined activity categories cover gas stations, private transport, public transport, flights, parcel/courier, water, electricity and gas. Preprocessing removes numbers/codes/symbols/diacritics/stop words, expands common acronyms and lemmatizes Spanish text. Linear SVC/RF use CountVectorizer unigrams/bigrams and chi-squared SelectPercentile; LSTM uses token sequences. [Verified §§IV-A, IV-B1–3](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.14505v1#S4.SS1); [local lines 300–413](../../research-papers/explainable-carbon-footprint-estimation.md#L300).
- **Defining XAI method:** LIME local model-agnostic feature selection identifies relevant input terms. When those terms contain enterprise names, explanations are enriched with up to ten frequent descriptors from the enterprise's processed description. A separate sector lexicon uses descriptions of six representative companies per sector (ten nouns/five verbs). Explanations are compared against sector vocabularies using Jaccard and a linguistic lexical/semantic proximity metric; automatic validation means the closest sector matches the classifier's predicted sector. This validates **semantic agreement**, not measured carbon, causal feature importance or the truth of the category itself. [Verified §III-C](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.14505v1#S3.SS3); [§IV-B4](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.14505v1#S4.SS2.SSS4).
- **Carbon estimator is not Trendl's MRIO estimator:** for fuel/travel/courier/utility categories, it estimates activity from payment `p` and representative price `avp`: `activity = p / avp`, then `CF = activity * epsilon`. Fuel uses average gasoline/diesel emission factor and average fuel price at transaction time; travel uses region/mode-dependent average price per kilometre; electricity/gas use average previous-month daily kWh prices. Water yields estimated **litres consumed**, **not carbon emissions**. These are price-based inferred physical quantities, not measured meter/receipt quantities and not full supply-chain MRIO footprints. [Verified §IV-B5, equations 1–7](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.14505v1#S4.SS2.SSS5); [local lines 515–575](../../research-papers/explainable-carbon-footprint-estimation.md#L515).
- **Verified metrics, rather than broad abstract shorthand:** ten-fold stratified CV reports SVC accuracy **93.72%**, precision **94.36%**, recall **92.34%**; RF accuracy **89.18%**, recall **86.36%**; LSTM accuracy **92.34%**. Thus “all three exceed 90% in all metrics” is not supported by the detailed table. These are paper results on its Spanish archetypes, not current repo scores. [Verified Table IV](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.14505v1#S4.T4).
- **Explanation denominator matters:** Table VI has SVC directly validated **48.13%**, obvious **9.96%**, coherent **15.77%**, empty **12.79%**, ambiguous **13.35%**. Summing the first three gives **73.86% satisfactory**; RF's corresponding sum is **71.78%**, directly validated **46.89%**. The conclusion/abstract's “60% automatically validated” shorthand must **not** be reported as 60% of all explanations; table values are clearer. Jaccard's RF direct validation is **34.85%**, linguistic metric **46.89%**. [Verified Tables V–VI](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.14505v1#S4.T5).
- The paper's cosine-distance dismissal invokes term order, but that statement should be treated as the authors' rationale, not a general mathematical fact: bag-of-words cosine similarity is order-invariant. A proposed replacement/extension must evaluate its metric empirically rather than reproduce that rationale unquestioningly. [Authors' §IV-B4 statement](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.14505v1#S4.SS2.SSS4).

### Inferences: concrete staged implementation plan

These recommendations prioritize the paper's distinctive **explanation mechanism**, without unnecessarily replacing an existing working logistic classifier or weakening fail-closed physical accounting. [Paper XAI contribution](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.14505v1#S3.SS3); [current classifier](../../src/greenpath/models/transaction_classifier.py#L284); [physical isolation](../../src/greenpath/extensions/uk_impact.py#L1).

1. **Stage X0 — expose auditable rule provenance (small, ~1 day).** Add a separate proposed `TransactionExplanation` contract in `contracts/transaction.py` and `TransactionCategoriser.explain` without silently changing `predict`'s three-column interface. Include original/normalized text references, rule/model version, rule ID, matched tokens/key, support/purity, predicted class and explicit “deterministic rule, not calibrated probability” label. Avoid duplicating private account numbers into exported explanations. #### Why physical counterfactual impact is not transaction-based footprint

| Dimension | Trendl consumption method | González-González transaction method | Current GreenPath physical impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Input | Categorized household spending | Transaction description/class and monetary amount plus representative prices | Explicit physical mileage/intensities/kWh/generation/performance assumptions |
| Arithmetic | `spend × MRIO category kg CO2e/GBP` | `(payment / unit price) × physical emission factor` | `baseline physical emissions − scenario physical emissions` |
| Quantity reported | Consumption-footprint level by domain/individual/household | Transaction-level estimated CO2; water is litres | Product-specific signed potential operational CO2/CO2e difference |
| Boundary | Consumption supply chains plus relevant direct emissions | Selected category formulas; not an independently validated full lifecycle inventory | Selected operational energy boundaries; manufacturing/embodied/end-of-life exclusions |
| Core validation need | Coverage/allocation/mapping and comparable survey distributions | Classification accuracy, explanation quality and price-inference accuracy | Physical evidence, same service demand/period, scope-compatible factors and scenario assumptions |

Table sources: [Trendl §§2.3–2.5](../../research-papers/carbon-footprints-from-financial-transactions.md#L316); [González-González §IV-B5](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.14505v1#S4.SS2.SSS5); [GreenPath calculation](../../src/greenpath/extensions/uk_impact.py#L76).
