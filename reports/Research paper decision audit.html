01 / Leakage and the Reproducibility Crisis in ML-based Science

Kapoor & Narayanan · 2022 supplied preprint

The most valuable paper here is about whether you can trust the result.

Direct accuracy improvement: none from writing manifests. Practical value: catches falsely good scores, stale deployments and unsupported claims before judges rerun the submission. This supports ML quality, architecture and Responsible AI.

Methods, implementation gaps, risks and acceptance

What the paper actually does

A taxonomy of eight leakage failure modes and model info sheets: clean splitting, legitimate features, independent samples, time ordering, and a justified target population. It is a methodology paper, not a new classifier.

What you have implemented

Substantial adoption: customer-held-out splits, train-fitted preprocessing, cutoff/availability controls, feature allowlists, merchant-group evaluation, and a reserved confidence-release cohort. The full source-to-artifact-to-report chain is not coherent in this workspace.

Your proposed plan, and my judgment

The assessment proposes final-manifest verification, feature/source info sheets, stronger noninterference tests, temporal contracts and paired uncertainty. I would do the manifest and feature sheet first; reserve deeper cross-fitting work for the calibration experiment.

Potential downsides

Extra reporting can become bureaucracy. Hashes prove byte identity, not feature legitimacy or population validity. A random customer split does not establish future-time performance. Do not claim the paper proves there is no leakage.

The increment I would build

Finalize one immutable run after every evaluator; include Git/dirty state, lockfile, inputs, splits, model/calibrator/release identities and outputs. Reconcile local confidence decisions with the saved report. Require every stage to verify against that identity.

What would justify keeping it

A clean checkout reproduces the same declared outputs; missing/mutated required files fail verification. Test-label changes cannot alter fitting or selection. Release-cohort changes cannot alter model fitting. Publish all feature-family availability and legitimacy assumptions.

What I would skip

Skip grand claims of “leakage-free” or “production validated.” Implement the argument and the checks.

Effort: Small to medium; temporal redesign is larger. These are scope estimates, not delivery promises.

02 / Classifier Calibration at Scale: An Empirical Study of Model-Agnostic Post-Hoc Methods

Manokhin & Grønhaug · 2026 · arXiv:2601.19944v1

Improve the calibration comparison before adding another calibrator.

One of the few additions that could directly improve probability estimates. Better probabilities help thresholding and cross-product planning. A monotone calibrator usually preserves ordering within a product, so do not promise better AUC or contact ranking.

Methods, implementation gaps, risks and acceptance

What the paper actually does

Compares five post-hoc calibrators across 21 classifiers on binary tabular tasks with separate calibration data. Beta and Venn–Abers are useful starting points, but no method wins universally. Proper scores can worsen after calibration.

What you have implemented

Binary Platt/isotonic are implemented. Two-fold validation predictions compare Brier scores, but automatic selection forces Platt below 50 examples in either class. The four saved core models have only 10–17 validation positives. Identity is not a selectable alternative. Model tuning and threshold fitting reuse validation.

Your proposed plan, and my judgment

The assessment proposes log-loss, identity, Beta/Venn–Abers and cleaner partitioning or cross-fitting. Adopt identity and paired Brier/log-loss reporting now. Try Beta only after selection/calibration independence is addressed. Venn–Abers is optional research.

Potential downsides

Sparse positives make selection noisy; isotonic can overfit and introduce ties. More methods increase multiple-comparison risk. Venn multiprobabilities are not customer-specific confidence intervals, and a merged point score does not inherit all theoretical guarantees. Distribution shift breaks exchangeability assumptions.

The increment I would build

Compare raw/identity, Platt and isotonic with the same clipping policy and nested out-of-fold selection. Predeclare Brier/log-loss tradeoffs. Add a constrained Beta challenger if enough effective data remain. Preserve untouched test and confidence-release cohorts.

What would justify keeping it

Choose without using saved test results. Freeze the pipeline, then report paired customer-level proper-score deltas, intervals, positive counts and shifted-data behavior. Keeping identity is a successful outcome if calibration loses.

What I would skip

Skip Pearsonify, a full 21-model benchmark, and “calibration always improves accuracy.” Never release correctness confidence solely because adoption probabilities are calibrated.

Effort: Small reporting change; medium protocol/candidate work. These are scope estimates, not delivery promises.

03 / Why do tree-based models still outperform deep learning on tabular data?

Grinsztajn, Oyallon & Varoquaux · NeurIPS 2022

Earn a model switch through your own task, not the paper title.

Bounded XGBoost tuning may capture interactions missed by logistic. Selecting for the RM workload may improve useful top-k recommendations. The saved EV candidate has higher P@10% but lower AUC than logistic; that motivates a new validation protocol, not a retrospective switch.

Methods, implementation gaps, risks and acceptance

What the paper actually does

Benchmarks tree and neural models on 45 tabular datasets, accounting for tuning, and studies irregular targets, irrelevant columns and feature rotations. Its curated regime excludes time-series and missing-data tasks and filters near-easy linear tasks.

What you have implemented

Heuristic, logistic and XGBoost candidates exist. All four saved core selections are logistic. Logistic gets four C values; XGBoost gets one fixed configuration. Selection uses validation ROC-AUC with raw-Brier tie-break; operational thresholds maximize validation F1.

Your proposed plan, and my judgment

The assessment proposes correct model naming, fair bounded tuning and a contact-capacity objective. Keep that plan, with realistic data/feature work before a large search. No neural benchmark is required.

Potential downsides

A tree can fit generator quirks. A small validation set makes tuning fragile. More candidates consume compute and invite test-driven choices. This benchmark does not prove trees should beat logistic on your small, synthetic, partly linear label mechanism.

The increment I would build

Declare whether the primary use is contact-capacity ranking or probability quality. Compare heuristic, regularized logistic and a small XGBoost search under recorded equal budgets; retain calibration, coverage, subgroup and missingness diagnostics.

What would justify keeping it

Freeze candidates and selection before a fresh held-out evaluation. Report PR-AUC, P@k/lift at an agreed capacity, proper scores, paired customer intervals, runtime and supports. Preserve the simpler model if gains are uncertain.

What I would skip

Skip tabular deep learning for novelty. Skip forcing XGBoost to make the SHAP paper sound implemented. Correct “HistGradientBoosting” wording to the actual XGBClassifier candidate.

Effort: Medium for fair bounded tuning and selection. These are scope estimates, not delivery promises.

04 / Explainable AI for Trees: From Local Explanations to Global Understanding

Lundberg et al. · 2019 supplied version

Useful explanations, with no automatic accuracy gain.

Improves inspection and explanation faithfulness, not prediction by itself. Can reveal suspicious missingness, synthetic shortcuts and dominant signals that inform a later validated model revision. Strong fit to FR7 and the 10% explainability criterion.

Methods, implementation gaps, risks and acceptance

What the paper actually does

Exact polynomial-time Tree SHAP, local interaction values, and global analyses assembled from local attributions. Applications include dependence, explanation-based grouping and performance monitoring.

What you have implemented

Exact XGBoost contributions and exact centered linear attributions are implemented, with missing indicators and raw-margin additivity checks. Saved selections are logistic, so saved explanations are linear SHAP. Offline export takes only the first 100 test rows. Runtime reason ordering uses logistic coefficient products, not the complete SHAP payload.

Your proposed plan, and my judgment

The assessment proposes global summaries, representative cohorts and optional interactions. Implement selected-model global summaries and connect faithful local evidence to explanations first. Interactions matter only if a tree wins selection.

Potential downsides

Correlated fuel/energy windows split credit according to the explanation game; imputation and background choice matter. Raw log-odds are not calibrated percentage-point changes, eligibility reasons or causal emissions effects. A visually attractive plot can falsely imply scientific validation.

The increment I would build

Aggregate the full held-out cohort or a documented representative sample. Show global mean-|SHAP|, signed distributions and a local example with baseline, negative terms, missing indicators and top-k residual. Version the selected artifact and background.

What would justify keeping it

Local contributions add to raw margin; global rankings recompute from the same persisted rows. No model/background/cohort mixing. Runtime claims explain the selected model and clearly state the output space.

What I would skip

Skip explanation-space clustering, calibrated-output SHAP and loss-attribution monitoring until they answer a specific question. Do not describe logistic SHAP as Tree SHAP.

Effort: Small to medium; interactions/loss monitoring larger. These are scope estimates, not delivery promises.

05 / Explainable Automatic Industrial Carbon Footprint Estimation From Bank Transaction Classification Using NLP

González-González et al. · IEEE Access 2022; arXiv deposit 2024

Borrow the transaction audit. Skip the spend-to-activity carbon shortcut.

Makes category errors inspectable before they corrupt fuel/energy spend features. Fuel recall of 0.135 and zero Home Improvement F1 on the saved unseen-merchant cohort are much more actionable than adding a new carbon widget. Explanations help debugging; they do not fix errors on their own.

Methods, implementation gaps, risks and acceptance

What the paper actually does

Classifies Spanish transaction descriptions, uses LIME-selected terms, merchant-descriptor expansion and sector-similarity checks. It estimates activity from payment divided by representative price, then applies factors. Water is reported as activity, not carbon.

What you have implemented

You have normalization, rule routing and TF-IDF/logistic fallback, but no transaction-token explanation path. Propensity SHAP explains a different model. Current physical carbon engines deliberately use physical scenario evidence rather than infer litres or kWh from spend.

Your proposed plan, and my judgment

The assessment proposes rule IDs/spans, exact fallback-feature attribution, optional seeded LIME/enrichment, and a separate price-proxy module. I recommend the first two; defer LIME unless exact features are insufficient and drop the price-proxy module for this submission.

Potential downsides

Semantic agreement is not category truth. Merchant expansion can fabricate meaning or inject labels. Character grams cannot be cleanly presented as unique causal words. LIME adds latency and instability. Spend/price ignores tariffs, standing charges, taxes, discounts, fuel mixes and multi-purpose purchases.

The increment I would build

Expose actual rule ID, matched span, fit-derived support/purity and deterministic semantics. For fallback, show signed TF-IDF coefficient contributions for the predicted-vs-runner-up logit, including intercept. Use only versioned, legitimately available merchant context.

What would justify keeping it

Review errors on independently authored or lawfully usable non-confidential merchant descriptions. Check route fidelity, attribution sums and privacy-safe output. Refit/evaluate downstream propensity after any category change.

What I would skip

Skip the amount/price carbon estimator. Your supplied activity tables are a stronger basis for FR4. Skip treating similarity validation as proof of carbon accuracy.

Effort: Small rule provenance; medium token audit; high proxy risk. These are scope estimates, not delivery promises.

06 / Explingo: Explaining AI Predictions using Large Language Models

Zytek et al. · IEEE Big Data 2024

Improve the explanation evidence; sentence shuffling alone is weak GenAI value.

Can improve customer understanding and fulfill the brief’s structured-output explanation role. It cannot improve the classifier or calculate impact. Because GenAI/LLM implementation is 15% of scoring, a measured improvement over a template is more convincing than calling a sentence permutation “personalization.”

Methods, implementation gaps, risks and acceptance

What the paper actually does

A Narrator converts explicit feature/value/SHAP tuples into text. A Grader evaluates correctness, completeness, fluency and conciseness; exemplar optimization improves narration. The study also identifies grading limitations.

What you have implemented

The provider may only reorder every approved server-authored sentence. Exact structured-field checks and deterministic fallback are strong. No SHAP tuples reach the narrator, no independent grader is deployed, and no live selected-provider quality result is established. Offline 200-case adversarial success tests final safety including fallback.

Your proposed plan, and my judgment

The assessment proposes faithful SHAP payloads, independent blinded quality comparisons, then optional narration/exemplar experiments. Do the payload and independent evaluation. Retain the fallback; do not expand free-form output simply to resemble the paper.

Potential downsides

Free narration may turn correlation into advice, omit negatives or invent eligibility. LLM grading can share narrator biases. The current reorderer adds network latency/cost without changing wording or length, so useful benefit is unproven. Grader self-agreement is not human comprehension.

The increment I would build

First compare current ordering against template-only presentation on blinded correctness, product mapping, missing/conflicting-data handling and comprehension. Add server-generated faithful attribution sentences. If necessary, experiment with constrained paraphrase separately, with validated output and fallback.

What would justify keeping it

Use held-out independently authored cases; separate raw, accepted and fallback outputs. Require correctness non-degradation and measured comprehension/readability benefit. Record actual provider version, latency, tokens and cost only if a live run occurs.

What I would skip

Skip DSPy/exemplar search and a second LLM judge until a basic comparison finds a useful gap. Skip “zero live hallucinations.” Do not count offline mocked rejection cases as provider quality.

Effort: Medium; independent human evaluation is the bottleneck. These are scope estimates, not delivery promises.

07 / Generating synthetic data in finance: opportunities, challenges and pitfalls

Assefa et al. · ICAIF 2020 · Review, not a prescribed architecture

Keep the mechanistic simulator. Improve its validation, not its buzzwords.

Better plausible variation and realistic text can improve robustness within the demonstration. Independent scenarios make evaluation less self-confirming. More rows mainly improve estimates of the simulator’s distribution; they do not establish real adoption behavior.

Methods, implementation gaps, risks and acceptance

What the paper actually does

Reviews financial data synthesis, realism/utility evaluation and privacy, including simulation, classical methods, GANs and differential privacy. It does not prescribe one universal generator or a single sufficient fidelity metric.

What you have implemented

Seeded longitudinal customer simulation includes adoption hazards, latent preferences, transactions, physical activity, missing/late data and perturbed configurations. Public energy anchors support limited plausibility; customer-product outcomes remain simulated. These are practical applications of the review’s lessons.

Your proposed plan, and my judgment

The assessment proposes honest generation provenance, broader utility/plausibility checks and only conditional private-data synthesis. Keep public aggregate anchors, entity/time constraints, stress cases and generator-shift testing. Private-data synthesis is outside the supplied competition data policy.

Potential downsides

A model can learn the generator rather than the business. Shared coefficients and templates create circular evaluation. Changing label rules to flatter a model invalidates comparisons. A GAN trained on generated data learns the same invented relationships and adds failure modes; “synthetic” does not imply differential privacy.

The increment I would build

Document all outcome assumptions; stress plausible alternate coefficients and missingness without optimizing the generator for scores. Add independently authored merchant descriptions and scenario labels. Validate joint distributions, rare categories, chronology and known-zero/missing distinctions, not just marginal histograms.

What would justify keeping it

A new frozen generator configuration passes invariants and maintains plausible source-anchored distributions; report utility changes across held-out configurations. Any measured predictive benefit must specify it is synthetic. Public electricity fidelity is not adoption-label validation.

What I would skip

Skip CTGAN, diffusion and DP machinery in this hackathon. The problem statement forbids real personal/confidential banking data; no source population is available to justify training a learned bank-data synthesizer.

Effort: Small to medium plausibility/stress work; GAN unjustified. These are scope estimates, not delivery promises.

08 / Scalable and Weakly Supervised Bank Transaction Classification

Toran et al. · 2023 · arXiv:2305.18430v2

A credible future option, but you do not currently have a label-scarcity problem.

Could use more unlabeled descriptions and recurrence patterns to improve unknown-merchant coverage. This is a plausible route to more reliable Customer360 spend features. With complete synthetic ground truth already available, weak labels discard information unless you deliberately study a limited-label setting.

Methods, implementation gaps, risks and acceptance

What the paper actually does

Groups normalized descriptions and account histories; labeling functions use text and spending patterns. A probabilistic label model combines weak signals, then trains discriminative models using transaction-trained embeddings and history representations. Anchoring expands text coverage.

What you have implemented

Rules produce final hard categories before a supervised fallback. Training labels come from generator truth for a sampled merchant reference. There are no abstaining LFs, learned LF accuracies, soft target aggregation or history-aware learner. Deterministic rule-first routing is not weak supervision.

Your proposed plan, and my judgment

The assessment proposes independent gold labels, LF diagnostics and simple soft-label distillation before FastText/GRU. Keep this conditional: pursue only if an independently reviewed non-confidential/public set exposes an actual annotation bottleneck.

Potential downsides

Correlated rules multiply the same bias; pseudo-label errors can be reinforced by the learner. Merchant/account histories can leak across splits or future cutoff. Independent binary tasks need a conflict resolver to satisfy exactly-one FR2 output. Sequence models add dependencies and latency.

The increment I would build

Create a fixed label-budget experiment. Compare supervised baseline, LF majority vote, probabilistic label model and a simple distilled learner on the same merchant-disjoint gold set. Keep all-abstain rows visible and report LF coverage, overlaps, conflicts and dependency assumptions.

What would justify keeping it

Predeclare annotation budget and practical per-category improvement. Use merchant-cluster uncertainty and a downstream propensity ablation. No holdout label may guide LF authoring. Include GAS support. A synthetic pilot may validate plumbing, not real label-efficiency claims.

What I would skip

Skip Snorkel/FastText/GRU as an automatic next step. Use no neural history stack until simple LF aggregation beats the supervised baseline at a genuinely constrained label budget.

Effort: Medium lightweight pilot; large faithful sequence stack. These are scope estimates, not delivery promises.

Your required taxonomy is flat. A hierarchy solves a different problem.

Potentially useful if the product later needs detailed consumption taxonomy and consistent rollups. That is not the current requirement. The transferable lesson is to obtain meaningful merchant context; making labels hierarchical does not repair missing semantic information.

Methods, implementation gaps, risks and acceptance

What the paper actually does

Transformer encoders process merchant name and business-activity text; fusion feeds macro/micro category heads. An inference-time taxonomy mask suppresses children incompatible with the highest-scoring parent. The paper’s results depend on richer context, not simply a larger encoder.

What you have implemented

One input text and eight mutually exclusive categories. No macro/micro relations, genuine business-activity descriptor or hierarchical labels. Flat boundary rules are compatible with FR2; they are not DragoNet.

Your proposed plan, and my judgment

Potential downsides

The increment I would build

What would justify keeping it

What I would skip

The improvement ceiling is partly a data problem. Take-up labels include unobserved preferences and random adoption noise. No Transformer, tree or calibrator can recover information absent from the available evidence. Changing the generator to make a learner win would manufacture an improvement.

How to prove an improvement

Start with a coherent frozen baseline. Change one mechanism at a time. Fit and select using train/validation or nested out-of-fold data, preserving the confidence-release cohort. Evaluate once on a fresh frozen test set. Resample customers for propensity and merchants for categorization; retain low-support and invalid-resample limitations.
